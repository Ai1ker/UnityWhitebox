# Recorded mechanical audio sources

These source recordings are retained unmodified for the game's quieter mechanical sound design. The playable clips in `Audio/SFX` may be cropped, layered, equalized, slowed, faded, or looped from these files. ## Scrapes — AntumDeluge

- Author and official asset page: https://opengameart.org/content/scrapes
- Permanent asset page: https://opengameart.org/node/81247
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/scrapes.zip
- License: CC0 1.0; the package's complete original legal text is preserved in `AntumDeluge_Scrapes/LICENSE.txt`.
- Recording description: an object dragged across a cinder block. Originally created for Stendhal and edited in Audacity.
- Retained files: `AntumDeluge_Scrapes/scrape-1.wav` through `scrape-8.wav`.
- Format: mono PCM 16-bit WAV, 44,100 Hz; about 1.14–1.57 seconds each.
- Intended use: grounded box scraping, foot scuffs, and short physical mechanism movements.

## Impact Sounds 1.0 — Kenney

- Author and official asset page: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/impact-sounds/87b4ddecda-1677589768/kenney_impact-sounds.zip
- License: CC0 1.0; the original package notice is preserved in `Kenney_ImpactSounds/License.txt`.
- Retained 21 OGG files: the `000`, `001`, and `002` variants of `footstep_concrete`, `impactMetal_heavy`, `impactMetal_medium`, `impactPlate_heavy`, `impactPlate_light`, `impactSoft_heavy`, and `impactTin_medium`, under `Kenney_ImpactSounds/Audio`.
- Intended use: physical foot impacts, articulated metal clunks, quieter latches, and exoskeleton parts falling. No electronic or science-fiction sound package was used.
