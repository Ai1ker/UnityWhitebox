# 图片生成提示词

方式：内置 ImageGen。墙面为不透明纹理，其余请求透明背景。均为新生成素材，原图已复制到本目录。以下为完整提示词：

## 墙面

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a seamless opaque 2D pixel-art laboratory wall texture for an actual side-scrolling Unity game. Create ONE square 1024x1024 texture tile, full bleed with NO transparent margins. Front orthographic elevation, no perspective or isometric view. A cold, deserted futuristic testing laboratory inspired by the restrained architectural simplicity of Portal and The Talos Principle, original design with no logos. Large flat cool silver-grey and off-white ceramic-coated metal wall panels, dark graphite recessed joints, restrained low-contrast shading, large calm areas. A repeating 2 by 2 grid of large rectangular panels, fine narrow seams crossing at the center. Each edge connects seamlessly to the opposite edge in both axes. A thin graphite seam at each tile edge, so neighboring copies form a consistent panel grid. Sparse aging: one or two small chipped panel corners exposing dark inner metal, a few short pixel cracks near a seam, subtle dusty discoloration low on one panel. Keep at least 85 percent of the wall surface clean and quiet. Damage is local surface wear, not gaping holes or broken silhouettes. Simple elegant graphic design, austere and abandoned, future technology and desolation together. Strict crisp readable pixel art with coherent block pixels, limited 8-to-12-color cool grey palette, no smooth painted or photographic texture. Medium-light grey panels around RGB135-175 and deep seams around RGB35-55. No saturated accent lights, no blue glowing circuits, no dense tech greebles, no wires, no pipes, no warning text, no symbols, no branding, no characters, no objects, no floor, no outer border frame, no vignette, no lighting gradients across the whole image, no dramatic shadows. It must be a usable tileable surface texture, not a room illustration or mockup. Opaque image.

## 完整核心箱（分层）

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a TWO-LAYER sprite atlas for ONE clean interactive physics cube in a 2D side-scrolling Unity game. One transparent PNG, landscape 2:1, ideally1024x512. Exactly TWO equal square 512x512 cells horizontally aligned, no grid or labels. Both layers have exactly matching center and scale for overlay compositing in Unity. STRICT front orthographic view, no visible top or side faces, no perspective, no isometric cube.
LEFT CELL: the intact square silver-grey laboratory CUBE OUTER SHELL. Square outer silhouette with only tiny chamfered corners, centered at256,256, bounds about40..472 on both axes. Simple elegant futuristic test equipment, restrained Portal / Talos Principle design language, original design. Four robust light silver corner braces, large cool light-grey planar faces, narrow graphite seams and a recessed dark circular socket at the exact center. Socket radius about64pixels. NO central glow on this left layer: leave the socket dark charcoal and neutral, ready for the separate luminous core to be overlaid. All shell pixels neutral grey/white/graphite, no colored stripes or lights. Minimal readable details, four small recessed bolts maximum. Completely clean, intact, factory-maintained; NO cracks, scratches, stains, chips, rust, dirt or damage. Crisp chunky pixel art with limited palette, hard pixel edges, simple low-contrast metal shading, NO smooth painterly gradients or photorealism.
RIGHT CELL: ONLY the small GLOWING CORE insert on otherwise transparent space. Center at768,256, perfectly circular luminous core with radius about52pixels inside a fine circular energy ring and a small soft alpha aura extending at most68pixels. This layer MUST be entirely neutral WHITE and light grey with transparent glow (not cyan, red, blue or amber): it will be tinted to arbitrary colors by Unity. A bright white central disc with a few pixel-cut details and a thin segmented white ring, restrained technological glow. Its size matches the left cell socket, so if the two cells are composited at the same center, the white core fits neatly in the dark socket. Absolutely no box, corners, screws, backdrop or other objects on the right cell.
Transparent background and generous consistent transparent padding around both layers. No shadows, floor, text, numbers, logos, hearts, emblems, arrows, grid or watermark. The shell should be visibly a compact physics box, not a humanoid robot or turret. It must remain very readable at a 1.15-world-unit game size.

## 单向平台

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE transparent pixel-art side-view modular platform sprite for a 2D futuristic laboratory game. Landscape 2:1 canvas ideally1024x512. A single very long thin HORIZONTAL straight laboratory walkway BEAM centered in the canvas, outer visible bounds roughly x48..976 and y192..320 (width928 height128), with generous fully transparent space above and below. Flat orthographic side elevation, NO perspective, no top surface seen in depth, no legs, supports, hanging wires or backdrop. Cool silver-grey minimalist metal casing, a very thin bright silver walkable top lip, dark graphite mechanical underside, two small squared end caps. A narrow understated pale CYAN light strip and a few evenly spaced small cyan dash lights across the front face signal that this platform allows drop-through. Neutral cold silver body, cyan only for the tiny lights. Original design matching clean silver laboratory robots. Sparse subtle wear on one end, otherwise uncluttered, elegant calm industrial design. Crisp chunky pixelart, limited palette, hard pixel edges and simple planar shading, no smooth painterly gradient, no detailed greebles. The middle section must be visually repeatable horizontally with a constant height; avoid large unique motifs that would reveal stretching. Both endpoints compact, all silhouette edges straight and readable. Only the isolated beam sprite with actual transparent alpha background. No text, grid, logos, arrows, warning icons, shadows, floor, characters or extra sprites. The beam will be sliced at its two endpoints and tiled across arbitrary platform lengths in Unity.

## 地刺

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE modular transparent pixel-art laboratory floor spike hazard for a side-scrolling 2D game. Square canvas ideally1024x1024, single hazard sprite, front orthographic side elevation. At the bottom is a low straight rectangular dark graphite mechanical mounting base, with a very thin dull silver upper rim. Exactly THREE evenly spaced tall triangular tapered silver-grey metal SPIKES protrude upward from this base. Clearly dangerous pointed tops, no rounded or blunt tips. Heights equal, simple narrow silhouettes, crisp medium-dark silver shading and a thin RED luminous seam along each spike. A few tiny RED warning status lights in the base. Cold, abandoned futuristic laboratory, austere restrained design matching a silver-grey pixel robot and simple panel walls. Small local chips or worn metal edges, no dense detail. Visible overall bounds roughly x0..1024 and y100..924, no lateral transparent padding on the base so multiple modules can tile horizontally edge-to-edge; fully transparent background elsewhere and above tips. Three spikes occupy most of the visible height, base only bottom12percent. Strict crisp chunky pixelart, limited neutral cool grey palette with red accents, no smooth illustration, no isometric or perspective view. No room, floor under the isolated module, characters, arrows, text, labels, logos, border, grids, cast shadows, gore or scattered debris. This is a practical tileable game hazard sprite and must read clearly against medium silver-grey laboratory walls.
